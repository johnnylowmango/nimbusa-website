6. Your Rights & Choices

  • Right to Access: Request to see all your personal data
  • Right to Delete: Request deletion of your account and data
  • Right to Correct: Update your profile information
  • Right to Opt-Out: Disable marketing emails, personalized ads, notifications, location tracking, analytics

Account Deletion

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently deleted:

  • User profile
  • Authentication credentials
  • All listings
  • Messages and chat history
  • Profile photos and uploaded media
  • Saved items and bookmarks
  • Ratings and reviews
  • Follower/following connections

Note: Payment records may be retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance purposes, as required by law. See the Delete Account page for details.

GDPR Rights (EU Users)

If you're in the EU, you have additional rights including data portability and the right to lodge complaints with your local data authority.

CCPA Rights (California Users)

If you're in California, you have rights to know what data we collect, access your data, delete your data, and opt-out of sales.
