Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

Tap "Sign Up" and enter your email address. You'll receive a verification email to confirm your account before you can vote or post.

Can I change my vote?

Yes. You can update your votes within the same quarter. Just visit the entity and tap "Rate" again to update your ratings.

What is a quarter?

A quarter is a 3-month period (Jan-Mar, Apr-Jun, Jul-Sep, Oct-Dec). You can vote once per entity per quarter.

Can I vote anonymously?

Yes. Check the "Post Anonymously" option when submitting your voice or verdict. Your identity will not be shown publicly.

How are scores calculated?

Scores are based on the balance of upvotes and downvotes across all categories, weighted by category importance and recency of votes.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account in the app under Profile › Settings › Delete Account. You can also email us at nimbusadev@gmail.com to request account deletion. See the Delete Account page for full details.
